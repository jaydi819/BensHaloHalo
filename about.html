Ben's Halo-Halo offers a variety of Filipino-inspired favorites for different cravings. The menu features coolers such as halo-halo and con yelo, refreshing beverages, rice meals, pasta, and pika-pika snacks.

Whether you're looking for a refreshing dessert on a warm day or a satisfying meal, there's a choice to enjoy and share with family and friends.
